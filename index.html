Why this matters

Government schemes like PM Vishwakarma and platforms like Amazon Karigar, Flipkart Samarth and GeM's Indiahandmade already move artisans onto marketplaces — but onboarding, photography and cataloging are still done manually by field staff, one artisan at a time. This app automates the highest-friction step (turning a raw photo + spoken description into a market-ready, priced, translated listing) so any artisan with a basic smartphone can self-onboard, and government staff can focus on training and dispute resolution instead of data entry.
